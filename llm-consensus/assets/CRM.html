Consensus bullishness: 0.95

What The Business Is

Salesforce Inc. is a large-cap enterprise software company best known for customer relationship management (“CRM”) applications delivered via cloud subscriptions. It serves sales, service, marketing, commerce, analytics, integration, collaboration, data-management, and AI-agent workflows across enterprise and public-sector customers. In Q1 FY2027, ended April 30, 2026, Salesforce reported $11.1 billion of revenue, up 13% year over year, with subscription and support revenue of $10.6 billion. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Salesforce sells a broad “Customer 360” platform spanning Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing, Commerce, Tableau analytics, MuleSoft integration, Slack collaboration, Data 360, Agentforce, and industry clouds. The transcript emphasizes Salesforce’s positioning around the “agentic enterprise”: AI agents embedded into workflows, Slack, APIs, and customer channels. Management cited examples such as PenFed using Agentforce for call summarization and operational agents, UCLA Health using Salesforce Health Cloud/Data 360/Agentforce for patient-facing navigation, and internal Salesforce deployments handling millions of inquiries. Salesforce also says Agentforce and Data 360 ARR reached nearly $3.4 billion in Q1 FY2027, including $1.2 billion of Agentforce ARR and $1.1 billion of Informatica Cloud ARR. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai)) AI adoption metrics were also prominent: Salesforce reported 3.8 billion Agentic Work Units delivered to date, up 111% quarter over quarter; more than 28.6 trillion tokens processed, up 152% quarter over quarter; and Slack MCP surpassing 1 million active users within six weeks. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai)) In June, Salesforce agreed to acquire Fin, formerly Intercom, for about $3.6 billion to add a customer-service AI agent platform across channels including chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, phone, and Slack. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/06/15/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-fin/?bc=OTH&utm_source=openai)) On July 7, Salesforce also announced a planned $1 billion investment in Switzerland over five years to support agentic AI transformation, workforce development, and local customer/partner growth.
