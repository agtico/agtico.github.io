CRM — SALESFORCE INC

Consensus bullishness: 1.49

What The Business Is

Salesforce Inc. is a large-cap enterprise software company best known for customer relationship management (“CRM”) applications delivered via cloud subscriptions. It serves sales, service, marketing, commerce, analytics, integration, collaboration, data-management, and AI-agent workflows across enterprise and public-sector customers. In Q1 FY2027, ended April 30, 2026, Salesforce reported $11.1 billion of revenue, up 13% year over year, with subscription and support revenue of $10.6 billion. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Salesforce sells a broad “Customer 360” platform spanning Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing, Commerce, Tableau analytics, MuleSoft integration, Slack collaboration, Data 360, Agentforce, and industry clouds. The transcript emphasizes Salesforce’s positioning around the “agentic enterprise”: AI agents embedded into workflows, Slack, APIs, and customer channels. Management cited examples such as PenFed using Agentforce for call summarization and operational agents, UCLA Health using Salesforce Health Cloud/Data 360/Agentforce for patient-facing navigation, and internal Salesforce deployments handling millions of inquiries. Salesforce also says Agentforce and Data 360 ARR reached nearly $3.4 billion in Q1 FY2027, including $1.2 billion of Agentforce ARR and $1.1 billion of Informatica Cloud ARR. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Salesforce delivered a strong Q1 FY2027 print: current remaining performance obligation was $33.6 billion, up 14% year over year; GAAP operating margin was 21.1%; non-GAAP operating margin was 34.8%; GAAP EPS rose 52% year over year to $2.42; and management raised the midpoint of FY2027 revenue guidance to $45.9 billion–$46.2 billion. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai)) AI adoption metrics were also prominent: Salesforce reported 3.8 billion Agentic Work Units delivered to date, up 111% quarter over quarter; more than 28.6 trillion tokens processed, up 152% quarter over quarter; and Slack MCP surpassing 1 million active users within six weeks. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai)) In June, Salesforce agreed to acquire Fin, formerly Intercom, for about $3.6 billion to add a customer-service AI agent platform across channels including chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, phone, and Slack. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/06/15/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-fin/?bc=OTH&utm_source=openai)) On July 7, Salesforce also announced a planned $1 billion investment in Switzerland over five years to support agentic AI transformation, workforce development, and local customer/partner growth. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/07/07/1-billion-ai-transformation-investment-switzerland/?bc=OTH&utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

Despite strong reported Q1 growth, the guidance mix leaves some scrutiny: Q2 FY2027 revenue guidance of $11.27 billion–$11.35 billion implies 10%–11% year-over-year growth, including slightly more than four points from Informatica, so organic growth acceleration remains a key execution test for the second half of FY2027. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai)) Salesforce’s AI narrative is increasingly central to the equity story, but the absolute Agentforce ARR contribution, while fast-growing, remains small relative to quarterly company revenue; that raises the bar for conversion of agent usage into durable revenue growth. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/05/27/fy27-q1-earnings/?utm_source=openai)) M&A is another watch item: Salesforce completed the roughly $8 billion Informatica acquisition in November 2025 and has now agreed to buy Fin for about $3.6 billion, increasing integration demands while the company is simultaneously repositioning around AI agents. ([salesforce.com](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2025/11/18/salesforce-completes-acquisition-of-informatica/?bc=OTH&utm_source=openai)) Market sentiment has also been weak: CRM was trading around $165.53 intraday on July 8, 2026, with market cap about $144.2 billion, well below levels implied by prior-year highs, reflecting investor concern over SaaS growth durability and AI disruption risk.
