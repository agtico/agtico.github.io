What The Business Is

Strategy Inc. (formerly MicroStrategy) is now primarily a **Bitcoin treasury company** with a legacy enterprise analytics/software business. Management describes the core model as raising capital through common equity, preferred equity/“digital credit,” and other instruments to accumulate and hold bitcoin over the long term. In its Q1 2026 materials, Strategy said it held **818,334 BTC** as of early May 2026, representing about **3.9% of the maximum bitcoin supply**, and characterized itself as the largest corporate bitcoin holder. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000105044626000024/mstr-20260505x8kxex991.htm?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Operationally, Strategy still sells analytics and application software, but the market’s focus is overwhelmingly on its bitcoin balance sheet and capital-markets engine. The Q1 2026 call excerpt emphasizes management’s objective to grow **bitcoin per share** via repeated BTC purchases funded by securities issuance, while keeping leverage “modest” relative to the bitcoin reserve. The company has also built a stack of preferred securities—STRF, STRC, STRK, STRD—positioned by management as “Digital Credit” products backed by Strategy’s bitcoin-heavy capital structure. ([assets.contentstack.io](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltf8d808d9b8cebd37/bltb120ca8ac738e134/69fa4a3250829712e58cb255/strategy-q1-2026-financial-results.pdf?utm_source=openai))
