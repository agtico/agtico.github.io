What The Business Is

CoreWeave is an AI-focused cloud infrastructure company, listed on Nasdaq as CRWV in March 2025, that sells access to high-performance GPU compute and related cloud services to AI labs, hyperscalers, startups, and enterprises. Management describes it as “The Essential Cloud for AI,” positioned between model developers and silicon suppliers by delivering infrastructure, software, and technical expertise for building and running AI at scale. ([investors.coreweave.com](https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Reports-Strong-First-Quarter-2026-Results/))

What It Does

CoreWeave rents and operates accelerated compute capacity built around NVIDIA GPUs, plus supporting CPU, storage, networking, inference, orchestration, and developer-tooling layers. The transcript emphasizes a shift from “just GPUs” toward an integrated AI cloud platform: customers use CoreWeave for training, inference, agentic workloads, cross-cloud connectivity, flexible reservations, spot capacity, dedicated inference, and Weights & Biases tooling. In Q1 2026, the company said over 90% of reserved-instance customers used at least two products, and it highlighted products such as Flex Reservations, Spot, Dedicated Inference, CoreWeave ARENA, and W&B Weave / W&B Models. ([investors.coreweave.com](https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Reports-Strong-First-Quarter-2026-Results/))
