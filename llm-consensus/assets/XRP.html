What The Business Is

XRP is the native asset of the XRP Ledger, a public blockchain associated with Ripple’s payments ecosystem. It is not equity in Ripple; rather, it is a transferable digital asset used for fees, liquidity, and settlement on the ledger. As of July 8, 2026, XRP trades around **$1.06** in the crypto market.

What It Does

XRP is designed for fast, low-cost value transfer, with the XRP Ledger supporting payments, token issuance, and liquidity functions. Ripple and related market participants continue to frame XRP around cross-border settlement and institutional crypto infrastructure, while U.S.-listed XRP ETFs now provide regulated exchange-traded exposure for investors that do not want to custody tokens directly. ([cryptoetf.today](https://cryptoetf.today/en/xrp-etf-flows?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Spot XRP ETF demand has been the clearest positive flow story: one ETF-flow tracker reports roughly **$1.4 billion** of cumulative net inflows and about **$1.38 billion** in AUM across four issuers, while CoinDesk reported that five U.S.-listed spot XRP funds took in **$25.8 million** on May 12, 2026, their largest single-day net inflow since January 5. ([cryptoetf.today](https://cryptoetf.today/en/xrp-etf-flows?utm_source=openai))

Regulatory infrastructure also appears to be expanding. A July 2026 market update reported that Ripple obtained a license enabling regulated cryptoasset services across the European Economic Area, adding to a stated base of more than 75 global regulatory licenses. ([coinstats.app](https://coinstats.app/ai/a/latest-news-for-ripple?utm_source=openai))

Supply dynamics remain somewhat predictable: Ripple’s escrow program continues to release up to **1 billion XRP monthly**, but reports note that unused amounts are often returned to escrow, reducing the effective net supply addition versus the headline unlock. ([lbank.com](https://www.lbank.com/explore/xrp-ripple-treasury-buyback-activity-decline-2026?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

ETF demand has not fully offset price weakness. Even if much is re-escrowed, the recurring unlock cycle keeps dilution and sale-risk questions active. Macro sensitivity is another negative driver.
