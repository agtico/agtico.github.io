COST — COSTCO WHOLESALE CORP

Consensus bullishness: 0.07

What The Business Is

Costco Wholesale Corp. is a membership-based warehouse retailer in the Consumer Defensive sector, operating large-format clubs that emphasize high inventory turnover, limited SKUs, low markups, private-label value, and recurring membership fees. As of fiscal Q3 2026, Costco reported **928 warehouses worldwide**, with management still targeting a longer-term pace of **30+ net new openings per year**, though FY2026 expected net openings were reduced to **26** from the prior plan by shifting two buildings into FY2027. ([fool.com](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/?source=iedfolrf0000001&utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Costco sells groceries, fresh foods, general merchandise, appliances, electronics, pharmacy, optical, tires, gasoline, travel and e-commerce offerings, primarily to paid members. Its Kirkland Signature private-label program is central to the value proposition, with management saying new Kirkland items are designed to save members at least **15%–20%** versus national-brand equivalents. The model also includes high-frequency traffic drivers such as gas and pharmacy; in Q3 FY2026, ancillary businesses grew comps in the mid-20s, with pharmacy and gas highlighted as major contributors. ([fool.com](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/?source=iedfolrf0000001&utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Costco’s fiscal Q3 2026 results were strong: net sales rose **11.6%** year over year to **$69.15 billion**, net income increased to **$2.19 billion**, and diluted EPS rose to **$4.93** from **$4.28**. Total comparable sales increased **9.8%**, or **6.6%** excluding gasoline and FX, while digitally enabled comparable sales rose **21.5%**. ([investor.costco.com](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-and-Year-To-Date-Operating-Results-For-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

Membership economics remained resilient: membership fee income rose **10.7%** year over year to **$1.37 billion**, paid members reached **82.9 million**, cardholders reached **149 million**, and U.S./Canada renewal improved to **92.2%**. Executive memberships rose **9.6%** to **41.2 million**, supporting management’s view that higher-tier members tend to visit and spend more. ([fool.com](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/?source=iedfolrf0000001&utm_source=openai))

The latest monthly data available before the July 8 scheduled June sales update showed continued momentum: April net sales rose **13.0%** to **$23.92 billion**, and May net sales rose **14.5%** to **$24.01 billion**. Costco’s investor site listed its **June Sales Results** event for **July 8, 2026 at 1:15 PM PT**, so that release was pending as of the current-date snapshot. ([stocktitan.net](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/COST/costco-wholesale-corporation-reports-april-sales-61fbtzoxocud.html?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

Some growth quality was less clean than headline comps: Q3 reported comparable sales benefited from gasoline price inflation and FX, while adjusted comps were lower at **6.6%** versus the headline **9.8%**. Gross margin also declined **21 basis points** year over year to **11.04%**, with management citing lower core margins in fresh and food/sundries from price investments, transportation headwinds from higher gas prices, and mix pressure from faster growth in gas, e-commerce and pharmacy. ([investor.costco.com](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-and-Year-To-Date-Operating-Results-For-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

Membership growth has moderated: paid members grew **4.1%** year over year in Q3, and management characterized **4%–5%** as a more normal rate absent major new-market openings or special-event demand. That could reduce one historical tailwind to same-store sales growth, even though renewal rates and executive-member growth remain strong. ([fool.com](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/?source=iedfolrf0000001&utm_source=openai))

Costco continues to face macro and policy uncertainty. Management flagged higher oil prices, possible longer-term inflation effects, resin-cost pressure in nonfoods, and tariff uncertainty; it also said Section 301 tariff refund claims were being submitted, with timing and amounts dependent on customs approvals and related litigation. Broader retail commentary in late May also pointed to value-focused but selective consumers, implying Costco’s value proposition is advantaged but not immune to household budget pressure. ([axios.com](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/28/retail-earnings-costco-kohls-best-buy-gap?utm_source=openai))
