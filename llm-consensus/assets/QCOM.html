What The Business Is

QUALCOMM INC (QCOM) is a semiconductor and wireless-technology company best known for Snapdragon chipsets and cellular intellectual property. It operates primarily through **QCT**, which sells chips and platforms for handsets, automotive, IoT, PCs, edge AI and emerging data-center products, and **QTL**, which licenses Qualcomm’s wireless patent portfolio. In its fiscal Q2 2026 call, management framed Qualcomm as a “connected edge to data center” AI compute company, not only a smartphone supplier.

What It Does

Qualcomm designs system-on-chips, modems, RF front-end, connectivity, AI accelerators, CPUs and software platforms used in smartphones, vehicles, PCs, wearables, industrial devices and networks. Its Snapdragon Digital Chassis targets cockpit, telematics, ADAS and automated-driving workloads; Snapdragon X targets AI PCs; industrial/robotics platforms target edge and “physical AI”; and the new Dragonfly portfolio targets data-center CPUs, inference accelerators, connectivity and custom silicon. At Investor Day 2026, Qualcomm said its Dragonfly data-center roadmap includes the C1000 CPU, High Bandwidth Compute, AI300 inference accelerator and custom silicon solutions. ([qualcomm.com](https://www.qualcomm.com/news/releases/2026/06/qualcomm-unveils-comprehensive-data-center-roadmap-for-the-agent?utm_source=openai))
