What The Business Is

Microsoft Corp. is a global technology company organized into three reportable segments: **Productivity and Business Processes**, **Intelligent Cloud**, and **More Personal Computing**. Its portfolio spans enterprise software subscriptions, cloud infrastructure, AI services, developer tools, business applications, LinkedIn, Windows, devices, search advertising, and gaming. In FY2025, Microsoft reported $281.7 billion of revenue and $128.5 billion of operating income, with Microsoft Cloud revenue of $168.9 billion. ([microsoft.com](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html?msockid=3db7fb97e7db61db1f89ed61e6d8602e))

What It Does

In **Productivity and Business Processes**, Microsoft sells Microsoft 365 Commercial and Consumer, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Teams, Exchange, SharePoint, Security and Compliance, LinkedIn, and Dynamics 365. In **Intelligent Cloud**, it sells Azure and other cloud services, cloud/AI consumption services, GitHub cloud services, Nuance, virtual desktop, Windows Server, SQL Server, Visual Studio, and enterprise services. In **More Personal Computing**, it sells Windows OEM licensing, Surface/devices, Xbox hardware and content, Game Pass, Bing/Copilot search and news advertising, and Edge-related services. ([microsoft.com](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html?msockid=09fe5543d85b694b22f743a9d91e684a))
