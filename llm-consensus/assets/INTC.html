What The Business Is

Intel Corp. is a U.S.-headquartered semiconductor company that designs, develops, manufactures, markets and services computing products and related semiconductor technologies. Its model remains more vertically integrated than many chip peers: Intel manufactures many of its own client and data-center products internally while also trying to build Intel Foundry into a larger external manufacturing and advanced-packaging business. Intel reports three main operating segments: Client Computing Group, Data Center and AI, and Intel Foundry. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000011/intc-20251227.htm?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Intel’s core products are CPUs and related silicon platforms for PCs, workstations, servers, cloud infrastructure, networking and edge systems. In client computing, it sells Core and Core Ultra processors, discrete graphics and connectivity/edge products. In data center and AI, it sells Xeon server CPUs, networking products, accelerators and related platform technologies. Intel Foundry provides internal manufacturing for Intel Products and offers wafer fabrication, process technology, advanced packaging and foundry services to external customers. ([stockanalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intc/company/?utm_source=openai))

A major current strategic focus is Intel 18A, the company’s leading-edge process node using RibbonFET and PowerVia technologies. Intel has positioned 18A as a foundation for both AI PCs and data-center CPUs, including Core Ultra Series 3/Panther Lake and Xeon 6+/Clearwater Forest. ([newsroom.intel.com](https://newsroom.intel.com/client-computing/intel-unveils-panther-lake-architecture-first-ai-pc-platform-built-on-18a?utm_source=openai))
