APP — APPLOVIN CORP

Consensus bullishness: 0.22

What The Business Is

AppLovin is a technology/software company focused on AI-driven performance marketing. It provides software and AI tools that help businesses “reach, monetize and grow” audiences, with a core historical base in mobile gaming and an expanding “consumer” advertiser vertical such as e-commerce and lead-generation advertisers. ([s21.q4cdn.com](https://s21.q4cdn.com/165405286/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/1Q26-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf))

What It Does

The company’s main platform matches advertisers with users across mobile game inventory using its Axon AI recommendation system. In late June 2026, AppLovin opened its self-serve ad platform to all advertisers, rebranding the advertiser-facing product from Axon to **AppLovin Ads**, while retaining **Axon** as the name of the AI recommendation engine; PocketGamer reported the platform reaches more than 1 billion daily active users through mobile gaming inventory. ([pocketgamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/applovin-opens-self-serve-advertising-platform-to-all-advertisers/)) Management said on the May 6, 2026 call that the platform had been closed for 14 years and that public access, AI creative tools, and agent-compatible infrastructure were intended to let advertisers onboard, generate ads, and scale campaigns with minimal human support.

Recent Highlights (Positive)

AppLovin reported a very strong Q1 2026: revenue of **$1.842 billion**, up **59% YoY**; net income of **$1.206 billion**, up **109% YoY**; adjusted EBITDA of **$1.557 billion**, up **66% YoY**; and an **85% adjusted EBITDA margin**. It also generated about **$1.3 billion** of operating cash flow and free cash flow, and repurchased/withheld **2.2 million shares** for **$1.0 billion** during the quarter. ([s21.q4cdn.com](https://s21.q4cdn.com/165405286/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/1Q26-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf)) Management guided Q2 2026 revenue to **$1.915–$1.945 billion** and adjusted EBITDA to **$1.615–$1.645 billion**, implying continued high growth and margins in the mid-80% range. ([s21.q4cdn.com](https://s21.q4cdn.com/165405286/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/1Q26-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf)) Operationally, management highlighted continued strength in gaming, accelerating consumer-advertiser spend, a material model improvement late in Q1, April consumer spend above prior Q4 peaks, and the June public launch as a major expansion of the addressable advertiser base.

Recent Highlights (Negative)

Key risks and controversies remain visible. AppLovin’s own Q1 release flags risks around forecasting, scaling the business to support new users, competition in advertising, macro conditions, and adapting to emerging technologies/business models. ([s21.q4cdn.com](https://s21.q4cdn.com/165405286/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/1Q26-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf)) Short-seller and compliance-related overhangs have also persisted: recent coverage noted AppLovin pushing back against a CapitalWatch report it called defamatory, while prior reports and investor investigations have alleged issues including money-laundering links, ad-tech compliance, or data-practice concerns; these are allegations, and AppLovin has disputed at least some claims.
