ETH — Ethereum

Consensus bullishness: 0.75

What The Business Is

Ethereum is a decentralized, proof-of-stake blockchain whose native asset, ETH, is used to pay transaction fees, secure the network via validator staking, and serve as collateral/settlement asset across DeFi, stablecoins, tokenized assets, NFTs, and layer-2 networks. Its “business” is best framed as selling scarce blockspace and data availability to applications and rollups, with ETH capturing value through gas payments, staking economics, and EIP-1559 fee burn. Spot ETH ETFs have made ETH accessible through U.S. brokerage accounts since July 2024, broadening institutional access to the asset. ([nerdwallet.com](https://www.nerdwallet.com/investing/learn/ethereum-etfs?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Ethereum provides a general-purpose execution layer for smart contracts and a settlement/data layer for scaling networks. Rollups post compressed transaction data to Ethereum using “blobs,” while Ethereum validators finalize blocks and earn issuance plus fee/MEV-related rewards. Recent protocol work has focused on scaling blob capacity and improving account abstraction/interoperability: ethereum.org notes Pectra activated on mainnet on May 7, 2025, and the Ethereum Foundation’s 2026 priorities emphasize blob-capacity ramps, Fusaka-related work, and UX improvements. ([ethereum.org](https://ethereum.org/roadmap/pectra/?utm_source=openai))
