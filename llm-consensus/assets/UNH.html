What The Business Is

UnitedHealth Group is a diversified U.S.-focused health care company with two major platforms: **UnitedHealthcare**, its health benefits/insurance arm, and **Optum**, its care delivery, pharmacy benefit, data/analytics, technology and health financial services arm. In Q1 2026, management said it had refocused on U.S. health care by exiting non-U.S. businesses, and reported **$111.7 billion** of quarterly revenue and **49.1 million** domestic medical members. ([unitedhealthgroup.com](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/content/dam/UHG/PDF/investors/2026/unh-reports-first-quarter-2026-results.pdf?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

UnitedHealthcare sells and administers employer, individual/ACA, Medicare Advantage, Medicare Part D, Medicaid and other health plans. Optum Health runs care delivery and value-based care operations; Optum Rx provides pharmacy care/PBM services; and Optum Insight supplies technology, data, claims and administrative services. In the April 21, 2026 transcript, management emphasized margin recovery over membership growth, value-based care discipline, AI-enabled modernization, and efforts to reduce administrative friction such as prior authorization. The company also said it planned nearly **$1.5 billion** of AI-related investment in 2026.
