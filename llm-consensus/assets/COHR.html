What The Business Is

Coherent Corp. is a global photonics and compound-semiconductor company serving communications, AI data centers, industrial lasers, instrumentation, electronics, and materials markets. The company describes itself as a “global photonics leader” with core capabilities in materials, optics, lasers, thermoelectrics, and networking products. ([coherent.com](https://www.coherent.com/company/about-us?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Coherent makes optical components, transceivers, lasers, engineered materials, thermoelectric products, and photonic subsystems used to move, generate, shape, and manage light. Its current growth center is Datacenter & Communications: 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers, ZR/ZR+ coherent modules, optical circuit switches, indium phosphide photonic devices, and co-packaged optics for AI data-center networks. It also sells industrial laser and materials products used in semiconductor equipment, manufacturing, medical, and scientific applications. ([coherent.com](https://www.coherent.com/?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Coherent’s May 6, 2026 fiscal Q3 report showed record quarterly revenue of **$1.81 billion**, up **21% year over year**, with non-GAAP gross margin of **39.6%** and non-GAAP EPS of **$1.41**; management guided fiscal Q4 revenue to **$1.91 billion–$2.05 billion**. On June 16, 2026, the U.S. Commerce Department announced a letter of intent for up to **$50 million** in CHIPS funding to expand Coherent’s Sherman, Texas 6-inch InP facility; Coherent said the project would double manufacturing space and quadruple wafer capacity.
