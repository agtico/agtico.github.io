LITE — LUMENTUM HOLDINGS INC

Consensus bullishness: -0.04

What The Business Is

Lumentum Holdings Inc. is a photonics and optical-components company serving cloud, AI data-center, telecom/networking, industrial, and commercial end markets. The company says it now operates as a single integrated enterprise rather than discrete segments, but it still reports product categories including **Components** and **Systems**. Its core exposure is to optical networking hardware that helps move data at very high bandwidth inside and between data centers. ([companiesmarketcap.com](https://www.companiesmarketcap.com/lumentum/sec-reports-10q/0001628280-26-030777/?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Lumentum makes laser chips, laser assemblies, transceivers, wavelength-selective switches, optical circuit switches, pump lasers, narrow-linewidth lasers, and other photonic products. In the May 5, 2026 transcript, management emphasized AI/cloud demand for **EML laser chips**, **CW lasers**, **1.6T transceivers**, **OCS**, **CPO-related lasers**, and “scale-across” components used to connect distributed data-center clusters. The company also announced a Greensboro, North Carolina facility to expand U.S. production of indium phosphide optical devices, including CW and ultra-high-power lasers for AI data-center infrastructure. ([investor.lumentum.com](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Announces-New-U-S--Manufacturing-Facility-to-Produce-Advanced-Lasers-for-the-Worlds-Largest-AI-Data-Centers/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Fiscal Q3 2026 was a record quarter: revenue was **$808.4 million**, up about **90% year over year**, with GAAP net income of **$144.2 million** and non-GAAP EPS of **$2.37**. Management guided fiscal Q4 2026 revenue to **$960 million–$1.01 billion**, implying another quarterly record at the midpoint. ([finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/lumentum-announces-third-quarter-fiscal-200300874.html?utm_source=openai))

The transcript points to broad AI-optics strength: Components revenue rose to **$533.3 million**, Systems revenue to **$275.1 million**, EML shipments set another company record, 200G EML revenue more than doubled sequentially, and cloud transceivers grew more than 40% sequentially. Management also said narrow-linewidth laser assemblies and pump lasers remained effectively sold out for the foreseeable future.

Lumentum’s balance sheet was boosted by NVIDIA’s strategic investment: cash and short-term investments rose by about **$2.0 billion** sequentially to **$3.17 billion**, with the increase primarily tied to issuance of Series A Convertible Preferred Stock in March 2026. Management said the capital supports capacity expansion for cloud and AI customers. ([investor.lumentum.com](https://investor.lumentum.com/quarterly-results/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

The Greensboro fab is a notable capacity-development milestone. Lumentum described the site as its fifth InP fab and said it will significantly expand 6-inch InP manufacturing capacity; local sources noted a 240,000-square-foot facility and more than 400 jobs preserved or created. ([investor.lumentum.com](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Announces-New-U-S--Manufacturing-Facility-to-Produce-Advanced-Lasers-for-the-Worlds-Largest-AI-Data-Centers/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

Supply remains a constraint despite strong demand. In the transcript, management said it was still “chasing behind” demand, with the EML supply-demand imbalance worse than the prior 25%–30% range, and pump-laser tightness even more severe. Transceiver shipments were also described as well below demand because of constrained electrical components and laser diodes.

Capacity expansion is capital-intensive and not immediate. Q3 CapEx was **$125 million**, mainly for manufacturing capacity, while the Greensboro facility requires conversion from gallium arsenide to indium phosphide; outside local reporting says volume production is expected to ramp in **mid-2028**, so it may not solve near-term shortages. ([chamber.greensboro.org](https://chamber.greensboro.org/news/Details/lumentum-selects-greensboro-for-new-us-manufacturing-facility-supporting-global-ai-infrastructure-321384?utm_source=openai))

Customer concentration and execution risk remain important. Lumentum’s Q3 10-Q states that revenue from customers outside the U.S. represented **77.0%** of quarterly revenue and flags risks from tariffs, export controls, supply-chain constraints, customer inventory behavior, and reliance on a small number of customers. ([companiesmarketcap.com](https://www.companiesmarketcap.com/lumentum/sec-reports-10q/0001628280-26-030777/?utm_source=openai))

Some legacy/non-AI areas are muted: in the transcript, management said performance industrial lasers were roughly flat sequentially and cable access shipments declined due to customer and timing factors, leaving recent growth heavily dependent on AI/cloud optics ramps.
