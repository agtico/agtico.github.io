META — META PLATFORMS INC

Consensus bullishness: -0.25

What The Business Is

Meta Platforms is a global social, messaging, advertising, and AI company in Communication Services. Its core **Family of Apps** includes Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and Threads; it also operates **Reality Labs**, which houses Quest VR, Ray-Ban/Oakley/Meta AI glasses, and broader metaverse hardware/software efforts. In Q1 2026, management said **3.56 billion people** used at least one Meta app daily in March, with a small sequential decline tied to Iran internet disruptions and WhatsApp restrictions in Russia. ([investor.atmeta.com](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-First-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Meta primarily monetizes attention and engagement through digital advertising across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and newer surfaces such as Threads. Management said Q1 2026 **Family of Apps ad revenue was $55.0B, up 33% year over year**, helped by 19% ad-impression growth and a 12% increase in average price per ad. The company is also embedding AI into ad ranking, creative tools, business messaging, recommendations, shopping, and Meta AI; Reality Labs contributes hardware and software revenue but remains much smaller than the ad business. ([investor.atmeta.com](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-First-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx?utm_source=openai))
