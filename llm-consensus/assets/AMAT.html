What The Business Is

Applied Materials (AMAT) is a Santa Clara–based semiconductor manufacturing equipment and services company. Its core business is selling materials-engineering systems, software and support used by chipmakers and display manufacturers; as of its latest reporting structure, its two reportable segments are **Semiconductor Systems** and **Applied Global Services (AGS)**. ([appliedmaterials.com](https://www.appliedmaterials.com/us/en/about.html?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

AMAT’s Semiconductor Systems business supplies tools for critical wafer-fab and packaging process steps, including deposition, etch, rapid thermal processing, chemical mechanical planarization, metrology/inspection, wafer packaging and ion implantation. AGS monetizes the installed base through spares, services, upgrades and software intended to improve ramp speed, equipment performance, fab output and cost. ([companiesmarketcap.com](https://companiesmarketcap.com/applied-materials/sec-reports-10k/0001628280-25-056742/?utm_source=openai))

Strategically, management is positioning AMAT around AI-driven semiconductor demand, especially **leading-edge foundry/logic**, **DRAM/HBM**, and **advanced packaging**. In the May 14, 2026 earnings-call excerpt, management said these areas are its “sweet spot,” expected them to drive more than 80% of year-on-year wafer-fab-equipment growth in 2026, and highlighted 8-quarter visibility from large customers.
