BRK.B — BERKSHIRE HATHAWAY INC

Consensus bullishness: 0.25

What The Business Is

Berkshire Hathaway is a U.S. holding company and diversified conglomerate whose core economic engine is insurance and reinsurance, supplemented by wholly owned operating businesses and a large public-equity portfolio. Its Class B shares trade on the NYSE as BRK.B; as of July 8, 2026, the quoted price was about $501, with a market capitalization around $701 billion. Warren Buffett remains chairman, while Greg Abel became CEO in January 2026 and led his first annual meeting in May 2026. ([investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/berkshire-shareholders-head-to-greg-abels-first-annual-meeting-with-buffett-in-audience-4654761))

What It Does

Berkshire’s businesses span insurance underwriting and investment income, BNSF railroad, Berkshire Hathaway Energy, manufacturing, service, retailing, and a concentrated portfolio of public equities. Its insurance operations include GEICO, Berkshire Hathaway Primary Group, and Berkshire Hathaway Reinsurance Group; the company also owns major non-insurance businesses tied to freight rail, utilities, industrial products, consumer brands, and retail. The first-quarter 2026 release described Berkshire and its subsidiaries as engaged in “diverse business activities including insurance and reinsurance, utilities and energy, manufacturing, service and retailing,” and listed BRK.A/BRK.B on the NYSE. ([mail.berkshirehathaway.com](https://mail.berkshirehathaway.com/news/may0226.pdf?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Berkshire reported stronger Q1 2026 operating results: operating earnings rose to about $11.35 billion, up roughly 18% year over year from $9.64 billion, with improvement across insurance underwriting and other core businesses. ([finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/berkshire-hathaway-reports-higher-q1-151900472.html?utm_source=openai)) Insurance underwriting was a key contributor, with underwriting operating earnings rising to about $1.72 billion from $1.34 billion, helped by improved results in primary insurance and property-casualty reinsurance despite a weaker GEICO combined ratio. ([theinsurer.com](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/news/berkshire-hathaway-grows-insurance-underwriting-earnings-28-to-172-billion-in-q1-2026-05-02/?utm_source=openai)) BNSF also improved, with Q1 earnings of about $1.38 billion versus $1.21 billion a year earlier, while Berkshire Hathaway Energy contributed about $1.11 billion, roughly flat to slightly higher year over year. ([finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/berkshire-hathaway-q1-2026-earnings-120012784.html?utm_source=openai))

The leadership transition appears operationally orderly so far: Abel presided over the May 2, 2026 annual meeting, with Buffett in the audience and still serving as chairman, while the meeting format shifted toward more detailed business-unit discussion. ([investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/berkshire-shareholders-head-to-greg-abels-first-annual-meeting-with-buffett-in-audience-4654761)) Berkshire also retained very high liquidity; reports around Q1 put cash near record levels, above the $373 billion year-end 2025 figure, giving management substantial flexibility even though deployment remains a central question. ([finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/berkshire-hathaway-q1-2026-earnings-120012784.html?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

The main overhang is capital deployment. Reuters noted that Abel inherited the challenge of investing Berkshire’s very large cash pile, after a decade without a “needle-moving” acquisition and after Berkshire resumed only modest share repurchases following a long pause. ([investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/berkshire-shareholders-head-to-greg-abels-first-annual-meeting-with-buffett-in-audience-4654761)) Q1 2026 repurchases were small relative to Berkshire’s scale—reported at roughly $235 million—suggesting limited buyback activity despite the cash balance. ([earningslens.ai](https://www.earningslens.ai/report/brk.b/2026-Q1?utm_source=openai))

Berkshire’s shares had materially lagged the S&P 500 since Buffett’s 2025 announcement that he would step down as CEO, with Reuters citing underperformance of 39 percentage points by the May 2026 meeting. ([investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/berkshire-shareholders-head-to-greg-abels-first-annual-meeting-with-buffett-in-audience-4654761)) Portfolio turnover also increased under the new era: in Q1, Berkshire was again a net seller of equities, buying about $16 billion and selling about $24 billion, while exiting or reducing a range of holdings including Amazon, Visa, Mastercard, Chevron, and others. ([kiplinger.com](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/stocks-berkshire-hathaway-bought-sold-q1-2026)) That activity may reflect discipline, but it also underscores a strategic transition from the Buffett-led investment model to Abel-led capital allocation, an area where investors are still assessing track record and style.
