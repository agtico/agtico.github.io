What The Business Is

JPMorgan Chase & Co. is the largest U.S.-based diversified bank by scale, with global operations across consumer banking, corporate and investment banking, commercial banking, payments, markets, and asset/wealth management. As of March 31, 2026, the firm reported **$4.9 trillion of assets** and **$364 billion of stockholders’ equity**, serving U.S. consumers and small businesses under the Chase brand and corporate, institutional, government, and wealth clients globally under J.P. Morgan and related brands. ([jpmorganchase.com](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/content/dam/jpmc/jpmorgan-chase-and-co/investor-relations/documents/quarterly-earnings/2026/1st-quarter/a5fd2d13-877b-43b2-8b58-81bad4399c87.pdf?hss_meta=eyJvcmdhbml6YXRpb25faWQiOiAxMTUsICJncm91cF9pZCI6IDU5MjQ2LCAiYXNzZXRfaWQiOiA0NjQ0NzksICJncm91cF9jb250ZW50X2lkIjogNzMwNTU5LCAiZ3JvdXBfbmV0d29ya19jb250ZW50X2lkIjogNzUzMzkyfQ%3D%3D&utm_source=openai))

What It Does

The business is organized around major franchises: **Consumer & Community Banking**—deposits, credit cards, auto, home lending, branches and digital banking; **Commercial & Investment Bank**—M&A/advisory, underwriting, treasury services, lending, fixed income and equities markets; **Asset & Wealth Management**—investment management, private banking, brokerage and retirement-related assets; and **Corporate**—treasury, capital, liquidity and other firm-level items. In the Q1 2026 call excerpt, management said CCB generated **$5.0 billion** of net income, CIB **$9.0 billion**, and AWM **$1.8 billion**, underscoring the firm’s broad earnings mix across retail banking, markets/investment banking, and fee-based wealth/asset management.
