What The Business Is

JPMorgan Chase & Co. is the largest U.S.-based diversified bank by scale, with global operations across consumer banking, corporate and investment banking, commercial banking, payments, markets, and asset/wealth management. As of March 31, 2026, the firm reported **$4.9 trillion of assets** and **$364 billion of stockholders’ equity**, serving U.S. consumers and small businesses under the Chase brand and corporate, institutional, government, and wealth clients globally under J.P. Morgan and related brands. What It Does

The business is organized around major franchises: **Consumer & Community Banking**—deposits, credit cards, auto, home lending, branches and digital banking; **Commercial & Investment Bank**—M&A/advisory, underwriting, treasury services, lending, fixed income and equities markets; **Asset & Wealth Management**—investment management, private banking, brokerage and retirement-related assets; and **Corporate**—treasury, capital, liquidity and other firm-level items. In the Q1 2026 call excerpt, management said CCB generated **$5.0 billion** of net income, CIB **$9.0 billion**, and AWM **$1.8 billion**, underscoring the firm’s broad earnings mix across retail banking, markets/investment banking, and fee-based wealth/asset management.
