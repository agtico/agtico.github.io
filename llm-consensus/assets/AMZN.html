What The Business Is

Amazon.com, Inc. is a global technology and retail platform company spanning online and physical retail, third-party marketplace services, Prime subscriptions, logistics, cloud computing, digital advertising, streaming/media, consumer devices, healthcare, autonomous vehicles, and satellite connectivity. Although classified here as **Consumer Cyclical / Internet Retail**, a large share of operating profit comes from **AWS**, Amazon’s cloud infrastructure and AI services business. In Q1 2026, Amazon reported **$181.5B** in revenue, up **17% year over year**, and **$23.9B** in operating income. ([ir.aboutamazon.com](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-First-Quarter-Results/?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Amazon sells first-party goods and enables third-party sellers on its marketplace; provides fulfillment, payments, advertising, and subscription services; operates Prime Video and other media assets; and runs AWS for compute, storage, databases, analytics, security, AI model access, and custom silicon. The transcript emphasized three main engines: **Stores** unit growth and faster delivery, **AWS** acceleration from AI and core cloud workloads, and **Ads** growth. AWS revenue was **$37.6B**, up **28% YoY**, while Amazon Ads revenue was **$17.2B**, up **22% YoY**, according to the Q1 call excerpt and Amazon’s Q1 release.
