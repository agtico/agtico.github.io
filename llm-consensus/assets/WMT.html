What The Business Is

Walmart Inc. is a global, people-led and tech-powered omnichannel retailer headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas, operating retail stores, wholesale clubs, eCommerce websites and mobile apps across the U.S., Africa, Canada, Central America, Chile, China, India and Mexico. Its reportable segments are **Walmart U.S.**, **Walmart International** and **Sam’s Club U.S.**; Walmart describes its purpose as helping customers “save money and live better,” serving about **280 million customers and members weekly** through **10,900+ stores** and digital properties in **19 countries**, with FY2026 revenue of **$713 billion** and about **2.1 million associates**. ([stock.walmart.com](https://stock.walmart.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000104169-26-000102/wmt-20260430.htm))

What It Does

Walmart sells grocery, general merchandise, health and wellness items, fuel and other categories through large-format stores, neighborhood formats, Sam’s Club membership warehouses, marketplaces, pickup, delivery and digital channels. In Q1 FY2027, Walmart U.S. net sales were led by grocery at **$70.7 billion**, followed by general merchandise at **$26.4 billion** and health and wellness at **$16.4 billion**; Walmart U.S. eCommerce sales were about **$27.1 billion**, while Sam’s Club U.S. eCommerce sales were about **$4.1 billion**. The company is also building higher-margin adjacent businesses including advertising, marketplace, fulfillment services, membership and data/commerce-media capabilities. ([stock.walmart.com](https://stock.walmart.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000104169-26-000102/wmt-20260430.htm))
