Consensus bullishness: -0.05

What The Business Is

Seagate Technology Holdings plc (STX) is a mass-capacity data storage company in the Technology / Computer Hardware industry, best known for hard disk drives (HDDs) and related storage systems. Its current strategic focus is high-capacity nearline HDDs for cloud, hyperscale, enterprise, and edge/IoT environments, where cost per terabyte, power efficiency, and reliable long-term retention are key purchasing criteria. Seagate’s fiscal Q3 2026 release described the company as “a leading innovator of mass-capacity data storage.” ([investors.seagate.com](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2026/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Seagate designs, manufactures, and sells HDDs and storage solutions, including high-capacity data-center drives. The company is scaling its Mozaic HAMR platform—heat-assisted magnetic recording—to increase areal density and drive capacity without proportionally increasing components. In the April 28, 2026 call excerpt, management said nearline products were close to 90% of total exabyte shipments in the March quarter, data-center revenue was about $2.5 billion, and cloud represented the majority of data-center revenue and capacity shipments. Seagate also said Mozaic 4+ can support up to 44TB per drive and that Mozaic 5 targets 50TB qualification shipments in late calendar 2027; its March Mozaic 4+ announcement similarly said the platform was qualified and in production with two leading hyperscale cloud providers. ([nasdaq.com](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/seagate-delivers-industrys-highest-capacity-hard-drives-next-generation-mozaictm-4?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Seagate’s fiscal Q3 2026 results, reported April 29, 2026 for the quarter ended April 3, were materially stronger year over year: revenue was $3.112 billion, up 44%; GAAP diluted EPS was $3.27; non-GAAP diluted EPS was $4.10; and non-GAAP gross margin reached 47%. Management guided fiscal Q4 2026 revenue to $3.45 billion ± $100 million and non-GAAP EPS to $5.00 ± $0.20. ([investors.seagate.com](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2026/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/?utm_source=openai))

The call excerpt highlighted strong AI/cloud storage demand: management said cloud customer revenue had grown for a tenth consecutive period, nearline capacity was almost fully allocated through calendar 2027, and build-to-order contracts were being finalized through fiscal 2027 with customer planning discussions extending into calendar 2028 and beyond. Management also raised its annual revenue growth target to a minimum of 20% over the next few years, citing AI-driven data creation, retention, and hyperscale infrastructure spending.

Cash generation and balance-sheet metrics also improved. In the transcript excerpt, Seagate reported $953 million of free cash flow in the March quarter, a 31% free-cash-flow margin, $1.1 billion of cash and equivalents, $2.4 billion of liquidity, $641 million of gross debt retired in the quarter, and approximately $1.1 billion of gross debt reduction fiscal year-to-date. The transcript excerpt notes that data center markets accounted for 88% of exabyte shipments and 80% of revenue in fiscal Q3 2026, implying business momentum is increasingly tied to a concentrated set of cloud and hyperscale customers, their capital budgets, qualification timelines, and long-term agreements.
