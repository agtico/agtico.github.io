What The Business Is

Robinhood Markets, Inc. is a U.S.-based financial-services platform focused on retail investors and active traders, with products spanning brokerage, options, futures, prediction-market event contracts, crypto, retirement, cash/banking, credit cards, advisory, and private-market access. In Q1 2026, management framed the strategy as becoming “#1 in Active Traders,” gaining wallet share with the next generation, and building a global financial ecosystem; reported Q1 total net revenue was **$1.07 billion**, up **15% year over year**. What It Does

Robinhood monetizes customer activity and balances through transaction-based revenues, net interest income, subscriptions such as Robinhood Gold, securities lending, crypto, credit products, and newer offerings including banking, prediction markets, futures, and private-market funds.
