What The Business Is

Exxon Mobil Corp. is a global, integrated energy company spanning upstream oil and gas production, refining, chemicals, specialty products, LNG, trading/logistics, and lower-carbon businesses. Its portfolio is anchored by large-scale “advantaged” assets such as the Permian Basin, Guyana deepwater, LNG, and global refining/chemicals operations. In Q1 2026, Exxon reported GAAP earnings of **$4.2 billion** and earnings excluding identified items and estimated timing effects of **$8.8 billion**, while emphasizing resilience through Middle East-related supply disruption. ([investor.exxonmobil.com](https://investor.exxonmobil.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000034088-26-000065/livef8k1q26991.htm))

What It Does

Exxon explores for and produces crude oil and natural gas; converts hydrocarbons into fuels, lubricants, petrochemicals, and specialty products; and uses its global supply chain and trading capabilities to move products to customers in more than 180 countries. The provided May 1, 2026 call excerpt highlights record Guyana production, planned 2026 Permian production of **1.8 million oil-equivalent barrels per day**, higher refinery throughput after turnarounds, Golden Pass LNG startup, and CO₂ transport/storage projects. Exxon’s 2026 climate/sustainability materials, issued May 5, discuss Scope 1 and 2 emissions ambitions, methane/flaring reduction, carbon capture, lower-emission fuels, lithium, and advanced materials. ([corporate.exxonmobil.com](https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/publications/advancing-climate-solutions?utm_source=openai))
