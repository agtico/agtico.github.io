What The Business Is

Lam Research is a Fremont, California–based semiconductor capital equipment company that supplies wafer-fabrication equipment and services used to make advanced chips; the company says nearly every advanced chip is built with Lam technology. ([investor.lamresearch.com](https://investor.lamresearch.com/2026-04-22-Lam-Research-Corporation-Reports-Financial-Results-for-the-Quarter-Ended-March-29%2C-2026?asPDF=)) Its business is tied to wafer-fab equipment spending cycles across foundry/logic, DRAM, NAND, advanced packaging, and installed-base support.

What It Does

Lam’s core systems revenue comes from equipment for deposition, etch, and other wafer-fabrication steps, while customer-support revenue includes service, spares, upgrades, and non-leading-edge Reliant tools. ([investor.lamresearch.com](https://investor.lamresearch.com/2026-04-22-Lam-Research-Corporation-Reports-Financial-Results-for-the-Quarter-Ended-March-29%2C-2026?asPDF=)) In the April 22, 2026 transcript excerpt, management emphasized that AI-driven device scaling is increasing etch and deposition intensity, especially in high-layer-count NAND, 1C-and-beyond DRAM, leading-edge foundry/logic, and advanced packaging. The company also highlighted its Customer Support Business Group, Dextro maintenance cobots, equipment-intelligence services, and a large installed base of more than 100,000 chambers as recurring support opportunities.
