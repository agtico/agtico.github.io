What The Business Is

Alphabet Inc. is the holding company behind Google, YouTube, Google Cloud, Android, Chrome, Waymo and other bets. Its core economic engine remains Google Services—Search, YouTube, Android/Play, subscriptions, platforms and devices—while Google Cloud is now a larger, faster-growing second pillar. In Q1 2026, Alphabet reported $109.9 billion of revenue, up 22% year over year, with Google Services revenue of $89.6 billion and Google Cloud revenue of $20.0 billion. ([s206.q4cdn.com](https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/2026q1-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Alphabet monetizes global consumer attention and intent primarily through advertising in Search, YouTube and partner networks; sells cloud infrastructure, data analytics, Workspace, cybersecurity and enterprise AI products through Google Cloud; and develops longer-duration businesses such as Waymo autonomous driving. The Q1 transcript emphasized Alphabet’s “full stack” AI approach: custom TPUs, NVIDIA GPU instances, Gemini models, AI Overviews/AI Mode in Search, Gemini Enterprise, and AI-enabled ad tools. Management said Search & Other revenue grew 19%, YouTube advertising grew 11%, subscriptions/platforms/devices grew 19%, and Cloud grew 63% in Q1 2026. ([s206.q4cdn.com](https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/2026q1-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf?utm_source=openai))
