What The Business Is

Corning Inc. is a 175-year-old materials-science company built around glass science, ceramic science, optical physics, and precision manufacturing. Its current markets include optical communications, mobile consumer electronics, display, automotive, solar, semiconductors, and life sciences. In Q1 2026, Corning changed its reporting structure: **Optical Communications** and **Automotive** remain standalone; **Glass Innovations** combines former Display and Specialty Materials; **Solar** includes Hemlock polysilicon plus solar wafer/module operations; and remaining activities sit in Life Sciences and Emerging Growth Businesses. ([corning.com](https://www.corning.com/worldwide/en/about-us/news-events/news-releases/2026/05/nvidia-and-corning-announce-long-term-partnership-to-strengthen-us-manufacturing-for-ai-infrastructure.html))

What It Does

Corning makes optical fiber, cable, and connectivity systems for carrier and enterprise networks; display glass for TVs, notebooks, and monitors; Gorilla Glass and advanced optics for mobile devices, semiconductor optics, aerospace/defense and other uses; ceramic substrates and filters for emissions control; life-sciences labware and bioprocessing products; and solar/semiconductor polysilicon, wafers, and modules. Its optical business is increasingly tied to AI data-center scale-out, while its Glass Innovations segment remains exposed to consumer electronics, displays, and semiconductor optics. ([corning.com](https://www.corning.com/worldwide/en/about-us/corning-business-segments.html))
