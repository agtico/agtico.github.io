NFLX — NETFLIX INC

Consensus bullishness: 0.40

What The Business Is

Netflix, Inc. is a global entertainment company in Communication Services / Entertainment. It operates one of the world’s leading entertainment services, offering TV series, films, games and live programming across genres and languages, with member plans that can be changed over time. ([ir.netflix.net](https://ir.netflix.net/investor-news-and-events/financial-releases/press-release-details/2026/Netflix-to-Announce-First-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)) The company’s core economic model is paid membership monetization, increasingly supplemented by advertising, pricing/plan optimization, live events, games, podcasts, licensing, and consumer-product extensions.

What It Does

Netflix acquires, produces, licenses, localizes and distributes entertainment directly to consumers through its streaming platform. Management frames the strategy around: adding more entertainment value; using technology and AI to improve discovery, delivery and production; and improving monetization through pricing, distribution partnerships and a growing ad business. ([s22.q4cdn.com](https://s22.q4cdn.com/959853165/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/FINAL-Q1-26-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)) In Q1 2026, Netflix highlighted expansion beyond traditional series/films into video podcasts, regional live events, a standalone kids gaming app, vertical video on mobile, and GenAI tools via the InterPositive acquisition. ([s22.q4cdn.com](https://s22.q4cdn.com/959853165/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/FINAL-Q1-26-Shareholder-Letter.pdf))

Recent Highlights (Positive)
