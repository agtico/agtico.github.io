PANW — PALO ALTO NETWORKS INC

Consensus bullishness: -0.30

What The Business Is

Palo Alto Networks is a large enterprise cybersecurity vendor in the Technology / Software Infrastructure category. It sells security platforms across network security, cloud security, security operations, AI security and identity; the company says it serves 70,000+ customers and is powered by Unit 42 threat intelligence. ([paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com](https://paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/palo-alto-networks-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2026-financial)) Its current strategy is “platformization”: consolidating customers from multiple point-security products onto broader PANW platforms, which management says improves retention and expansion.

What It Does

PANW’s portfolio includes next-generation firewalls, SASE, software firewalls, Cortex/XSIAM for security operations, Prisma AIRS for AI security, cloud security, and—after the CyberArk deal—identity security. In the June 2, 2026 earnings-call excerpt, management emphasized that AI workloads are increasing machine-to-machine traffic, identity exposure, and attack speed, which PANW argues raises demand for inline inspection, automated SOC response, and agent/identity governance. Recent acquisitions extend this stack: Koi is intended to add “agentic endpoint” security to Prisma AIRS and Cortex XDR, while Portkey adds an AI gateway control plane for monitoring, orchestrating, and governing autonomous agents at scale. ([paloaltonetworks.com](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/company/press/2026/palo-alto-networks-announces-intent-to-acquire-koi-to-secure-the-agentic-endpoint)) ([paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com](https://paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/palo-alto-networks-completes-acquisition-portkey-secure-ai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

PANW reported a record fiscal Q3 2026: total revenue rose 31% year over year to $3.0 billion, and next-generation security ARR reached $8.13 billion, up 60% year over year, including $1.63 billion from CyberArk and Chronosphere. ([paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com](https://paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/palo-alto-networks-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2026-financial)) Management also said organic NGS ARR rose 28% and organic RPO rose 22%, with total RPO at $18.4 billion. The transcript highlighted broad demand: SASE ARR of $1.6 billion, up 40%; XSIAM ARR above $600 million, up 100%; Prisma AIRS surpassing 300 customers; and nearly 40% year-over-year growth in next-generation firewall bookings. PANW also closed Portkey on May 29, 2026, shortly before the earnings call, positioning the deal as a way to expand Prisma AIRS into AI-agent governance and token-scale AI traffic control. ([paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com](https://paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/palo-alto-networks-completes-acquisition-portkey-secure-ai))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

GAAP profitability was weaker in Q3 despite strong top-line growth: PANW reported a GAAP operating loss of $183 million versus operating income of $219 million a year earlier, and a GAAP net loss of $177 million versus net income of $262 million a year earlier. ([paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com](https://paloaltonetworks.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/palo-alto-networks-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2026-financial)) Acquisition complexity is also material: the June 2026 10-Q shows Koi closed on April 14, 2026; Portkey was agreed on April 30 for $140 million of cash and replacement awards; and Chronosphere plus CyberArk contributed $388 million of revenue but a $523 million operating loss since acquisition dates in the quarter. ([investors.paloaltonetworks.com](https://investors.paloaltonetworks.com/static-files/8326c161-2486-49f1-8d63-38213de7400a)) PANW’s own Koi disclosure flags typical deal risks, including integration difficulties, management distraction, unanticipated liabilities or expenditures, and uncertainty around realizing synergies. ([paloaltonetworks.com](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/company/press/2026/palo-alto-networks-announces-intent-to-acquire-koi-to-secure-the-agentic-endpoint))
