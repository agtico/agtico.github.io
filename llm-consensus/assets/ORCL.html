What The Business Is

Oracle Corp. is a global enterprise technology company centered on databases, cloud infrastructure, enterprise applications, and industry software. It is now led by co-CEOs Clay Magouyrk and Mike Sicilia, with Safra Catz having moved to executive vice chair in September 2025. ([investor.oracle.com](https://investor.oracle.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/Oracle-Corporation-Announces-Promotion-of-Clay-Magouyrk-and-Mike-Sicilia-to-CEOs-Safra-Catz-Appointed-Executive-Vice-Chair-of-the-Board-of-Directors/default.aspx?utm_source=openai)) Oracle’s current strategic identity is increasingly “full stack”: OCI cloud infrastructure for AI and enterprise workloads, Oracle Database and related data services, and SaaS applications such as Fusion ERP/HCM/SCM/CX, NetSuite, and Oracle Health.

What It Does

Oracle sells cloud and license support, cloud infrastructure, database services, enterprise applications, hardware, and services. OCI competes with hyperscale clouds while emphasizing high-performance networking, database-native workloads, sovereign/dedicated regions, and large AI clusters. Oracle also embeds AI into applications: in the Q4 FY2026 transcript, management said it had delivered more than 1,000 AI agents across application suites and is testing token and outcome-based pricing for more advanced agentic use cases. Recent product news included Fusion Agentic Applications for CX and a builder in Oracle AI Agent Studio for connecting Oracle, partner, and external agents. ([prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oracle-introduces-fusion-agentic-applications-for-customer-experience-302737686.html?utm_source=openai)) Oracle also continues to push multicloud database access through offerings with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud; in April, Oracle and AWS announced plans to expand private multicloud networking between OCI and AWS. ([prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/oracle-and-aws-collaborate-to-expand-multicloud-networking-302743862.html?utm_source=openai))
