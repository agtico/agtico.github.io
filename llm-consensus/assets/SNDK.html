Consensus bullishness: -0.04

What The Business Is

Sandisk Corporation (SNDK) is a NAND flash storage company in Technology / Computer Hardware. It develops, manufactures and sells flash-based storage devices and solutions, including solid-state drives, embedded products, removable cards, USB drives, wafers and components, serving consumer, edge/client and data-center markets. ([investor.sandisk.com](https://investor.sandisk.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sandisk-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2026-financial-results))

What It Does

Sandisk’s core products are NAND-based storage components and systems used to capture, preserve, access and process data across PCs, smartphones, enterprise servers, public cloud and AI infrastructure. Management’s April 30, 2026 call emphasized a shift toward higher-value data-center SSDs, including TLC enterprise SSDs for performance-intensive workloads and planned QLC “Stargate” revenue shipments in fiscal Q4. The company also relies on long-term manufacturing and technology collaboration with Kioxia and has added DRAM supply resiliency via a Nanya-related investment, per the transcript excerpt and 10-Q exhibit references. ([investor.sandisk.com](https://investor.sandisk.com/static-files/21ecbf29-74ac-4abd-9563-2afd618ddbba))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Fiscal Q3 2026 was a major beat: revenue was **$5.95 billion**, up **97% sequentially** and above guidance, with GAAP net income of **$3.615 billion** and non-GAAP EPS of **$23.41**; data-center revenue rose **233% sequentially**, and fiscal Q4 guidance called for **$7.75 billion–$8.25 billion** of revenue and non-GAAP EPS of **$30–$33**. ([sandisk.com](https://www.sandisk.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/2026-04-30-sandisk-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2026-financial-results?utm_source=openai)) Management also disclosed five signed multiyear “new business model” agreements by early fiscal Q4; the transcript says the first three contracts create about **$42 billion** of minimum contractual revenue, and all five include financial guarantees exceeding **$11 billion**, aimed at reducing NAND cyclicality.

Capital returns also moved into focus: on the Q3 call, management said it had repaid the remaining term-loan balance, reached a net-cash target, and authorized a **$6 billion** share-repurchase program. Product-roadmap news followed: on **July 2, 2026**, Sandisk announced sampling of **BiCS10 1Tb TLC 3D NAND**, claiming over **29 Gb/mm²** density, **59%** better bit density versus BiCS8, up to **4.8 Gb/s** interface speed and improved I/O power efficiency. ([sandisk.com](https://www.sandisk.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/2026-07-02-sandisk-announces-bics10-1tb-tlc))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

The same long-term agreements that improve visibility add execution risk. Sandisk’s May 1, 2026 10-Q warns that failure to deliver required quantities, timing or specifications under multiyear contracts could trigger pricing or volume reductions, damages, penalties or termination; if customers fail to perform, financial guarantees may offset only part of lost revenue. ([investor.sandisk.com](https://investor.sandisk.com/static-files/21ecbf29-74ac-4abd-9563-2afd618ddbba))

The Q3 results also reflect unusually strong NAND pricing and mix: non-GAAP gross margin reached **78.4%** in the transcript, far above typical memory-cycle levels, making future results sensitive to NAND average selling prices, customer mix and AI/data-center demand durability. Company risk language also highlights volatile demand, ASP fluctuations, competitive pricing, supply-chain disruption, reliance on key partners including Kioxia, and customer relationship or customer-base consolidation risks. ([investor.sandisk.com](https://investor.sandisk.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sandisk-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2026-financial-results)) Consumer revenue was weaker sequentially in Q3, down **10%** per the transcript, which management described as seasonal but still shows not all end markets are accelerating at the same pace. Sandisk also publicly told holders on **May 14, 2026** to reject a “mini-tender” offer, indicating a shareholder-protection issue around third-party offer terms rather than an operating development. ([investor.sandisk.com](https://investor.sandisk.com/))
