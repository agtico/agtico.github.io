AAOI — APPLIED OPTOELECTRONICS INC

Consensus bullishness: -0.08

What The Business Is

Applied Optoelectronics, Inc. is a Sugar Land, Texas–based optical networking company serving hyperscale data centers, cable TV/HFC networks, telecom, FTTH and related markets. Its business is increasingly tied to AI data-center buildouts, where high-speed optical links are needed inside and between cloud infrastructure; in 2025, revenue was mainly split between CATV and internet data-center markets. ([investors.ao-inc.com](https://investors.ao-inc.com/static-files/6e93dd0b-5f30-4019-9855-643c1458d94f?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

AAOI designs, manufactures and sells optical modules, lasers, components, subassemblies, transmitters/transceivers and HFC/CATV networking equipment. In data centers, it supplies pluggable optical transceivers for switches and servers, including 100G, 400G, 800G and emerging 1.6T products. In CATV, it sells amplifiers and related HFC gear, including products under its Quantum Bandwidth brand. Management emphasizes in-house laser manufacturing and automated transceiver production across U.S., Taiwan and China facilities. ([investors.ao-inc.com](https://investors.ao-inc.com/static-files/6e93dd0b-5f30-4019-9855-643c1458d94f?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Positive)

AAOI’s May 7, 2026 Q1 report showed record revenue of **$151.1 million**, up **51% year over year** and **13% sequentially**, with non-GAAP gross margin of **29.2%** and non-GAAP EPS loss of **$0.07**, in line with guidance. Management guided Q2 revenue to **$180 million–$198 million** and raised its 2026 outlook to **more than $1.1 billion** of revenue and **more than $140 million** of non-GAAP operating income. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158114/000168316826003562/aaoi_ex9901.htm?utm_source=openai))

The transcript highlights accelerating demand for AI-related optics: AAOI completed its first volume shipment of 800G single-mode transceivers to a large hyperscale customer in Q1, expected nearly **4x** higher 800G unit shipments in Q2 versus Q1, and said forecast demand for 800G/1.6T modules is expected to exceed production capacity through mid-2027. The company also disclosed its first volume order for 1.6T data-center transceivers from a long-term hyperscale customer, with 1.6T deliveries potentially beginning as early as Q3 2026. ([investors.ao-inc.com](https://investors.ao-inc.com/news-releases/news-release-details/aoi-receives-first-volume-order-16t-data-center-transceivers?utm_source=openai))

Capacity expansion is a central positive development. Management said its Texas manufacturing footprint has expanded to about **900,000 square feet**, with a new 210,000-square-foot facility dedicated to 800G/1.6T transceivers expected to start initial production in Q3 2026. It targeted more than **650,000** combined 800G/1.6T units per month by year-end 2026 and more than **930,000** per month by year-end 2027, with increasing U.S. output. ([roic.ai](https://www.roic.ai/quote/AAOI%3AUS/transcripts/2026-year/1-quarter?utm_source=openai))

Recent Highlights (Negative)

Despite revenue growth, AAOI remained unprofitable in Q1 on a GAAP basis, reporting a **$14.3 million net loss**, or **$0.19 per basic share**; non-GAAP operating loss was **$7.3 million**. Gross margin also declined versus Q4 2025 and Q1 2025, and management noted near-term data-center mix could remain a slight gross-margin headwind. ([sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1158114/000168316826003562/aaoi_ex9901.htm?utm_source=openai))

Execution risk is elevated because the 2026 outlook depends on rapid qualification, manufacturing ramp and capacity additions. Q1 capital investments were **$68.7 million**, mainly for 400G/800G/1.6T capacity, and management said quarterly capex is expected to run above Q1 levels, funded through cash, operating cash flow, equity sales and additional debt. Inventory also rose to **$206.2 million** as of March 31, 2026, reflecting raw material and work-in-process buildup for production. ([roic.ai](https://www.roic.ai/quote/AAOI%3AUS/transcripts/2026-year/1-quarter?utm_source=openai))

Customer concentration remains a material risk. In Q1, the top 10 customers represented **98%** of revenue, with three customers each above 10%: one CATV customer at **44%** of total revenue and two data-center customers at **26%** and **25%**. The company also recognizes contra-revenue tied to customer warrants, including about **$1.0 million** in Q1 2026, which complicates headline revenue quality and economics for certain customer arrangements. ([roic.ai](https://www.roic.ai/quote/AAOI%3AUS/transcripts/2026-year/1-quarter?utm_source=openai))
