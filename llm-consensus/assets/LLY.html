Consensus bullishness: 0.28

What The Business Is

Eli Lilly & Co. is a global pharmaceutical company focused on branded, patent-protected medicines across cardiometabolic disease, diabetes/obesity, oncology, immunology and neuroscience. Its current growth profile is heavily tied to incretin therapies, especially Mounjaro for type 2 diabetes, Zepbound for obesity, and the newly approved oral GLP-1 Foundayo/orforglipron. In Q1 2026, Lilly reported revenue of $19.8 billion, up 56% year over year, and raised 2026 revenue guidance to $82 billion–$85 billion. ([investor.lilly.com](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-reports-first-quarter-2026-financial-results-raises-full?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Lilly discovers, develops, manufactures and commercializes prescription medicines. Its largest franchise is cardiometabolic: Mounjaro, Zepbound and Foundayo are GLP-1/incretin-based therapies aimed at diabetes, obesity and related conditions. The company also markets and develops drugs in immunology, oncology and neuroscience, including Alzheimer’s and cancer assets. Per the April 30 transcript, management highlighted 42 active Phase III programs, expanding GLP-1 access through LillyDirect, employer channels and Medicare bridge access, and ongoing reviews for Foundayo in more than 40 countries.

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Lilly received FDA approval on April 1, 2026 for Foundayo, an oral GLP-1 pill for chronic weight management that can be taken without food or water restrictions; FDA’s novel-drug list confirms the indication for adults with obesity or overweight with at least one weight-related comorbidity. ([investor.lilly.com](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fda-approves-lillys-foundayotm-orforglipron-only-glp-1-pill?utm_source=openai)) The company also announced that all three of the largest U.S. PBMs would cover its obesity portfolio, and later detailed Medicare GLP-1 Bridge access beginning July 1, 2026, with eligible Part D patients able to access Foundayo or Zepbound for $50/month. ([investor.lilly.com](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/foundayo-and-zepbound-now-covered-millions-americans?utm_source=openai)) Pipeline momentum was material: retatrutide delivered positive Phase 3 obesity results, with Lilly reporting up to 28.3% average body-weight reduction at 80 weeks in TRIUMPH-1; Axios described the result as approaching bariatric-surgery-level weight loss. ([investor.lilly.com](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-triple-agonist-retatrutide-delivered-powerful-weight-loss?utm_source=openai)) The Q1 call also cited positive Phase III readouts in diabetes, CLL, pediatric atopic dermatitis, psoriasis/obesity combination treatment, and new Phase III starts across obesity-related complications and neuroscience.

Recent Highlights (Negative)

Despite strong volume growth, pricing pressure remains visible: Lilly said Q1 U.S. revenue growth was partly offset by a 7% decline from lower realized prices, primarily driven by Zepbound and Taltz, and the transcript noted expected full-year price headwinds in the low-to-mid teens. ([investor.lilly.com](https://investor.lilly.com/node/54176?utm_source=openai)) Gross margin also slipped about 1 percentage point year over year to 82.6%, while R&D rose 28% and selling/marketing/admin rose 19%, reflecting heavy launch and pipeline spending. ([fool.com](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/30/eli-lilly-lly-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/?utm_source=openai)) Regulatory and policy friction around drug pricing persists: 340B disputes involving Lilly escalated, with hospital groups criticizing Lilly’s policy changes and lawmakers urging HHS to force Lilly to provide discounts to hospitals. ([aha.org](https://www.aha.org/2026-01-26-aha-urges-hrsa-stop-eli-lillys-new-policy-340b-hospitals-going-effect?utm_source=openai)) GLP-1 access remains operationally sensitive: FDA has been clarifying compounding policies as GLP-1 supply stabilizes, and even post-shortage, patients and pharmacies can face dose-specific availability issues, creating execution risk around very high demand. ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-clarifies-policies-compounders-national-glp-1-supply-begins-stabilize?utm_source=openai))
