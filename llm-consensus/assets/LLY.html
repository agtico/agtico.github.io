What The Business Is

Eli Lilly & Co. is a global pharmaceutical company focused on branded, patent-protected medicines across cardiometabolic disease, diabetes/obesity, oncology, immunology and neuroscience. Its current growth profile is heavily tied to incretin therapies, especially Mounjaro for type 2 diabetes, Zepbound for obesity, and the newly approved oral GLP-1 Foundayo/orforglipron. In Q1 2026, Lilly reported revenue of $19.8 billion, up 56% year over year, and raised 2026 revenue guidance to $82 billion–$85 billion. ([investor.lilly.com](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-reports-first-quarter-2026-financial-results-raises-full?utm_source=openai))

What It Does

Lilly discovers, develops, manufactures and commercializes prescription medicines. Its largest franchise is cardiometabolic: Mounjaro, Zepbound and Foundayo are GLP-1/incretin-based therapies aimed at diabetes, obesity and related conditions. The company also markets and develops drugs in immunology, oncology and neuroscience, including Alzheimer’s and cancer assets. Per the April 30 transcript, management highlighted 42 active Phase III programs, expanding GLP-1 access through LillyDirect, employer channels and Medicare bridge access, and ongoing reviews for Foundayo in more than 40 countries.

Recent Highlights (Positive)

Lilly received FDA approval on April 1, 2026 for Foundayo, an oral GLP-1 pill for chronic weight management that can be taken without food or water restrictions; FDA’s novel-drug list confirms the indication for adults with obesity or overweight with at least one weight-related comorbidity.
